Analyse tab: Log on OK, the other states, and the wide layout

Log on OK is something you start and stop. The Log on OK button sits at the right of the "Save analysis" heading (the phone's bottom bar holds Save, Save & print and the copies). Once started, the bottom bar shows it running, with Stop. The crops below show only the parts that change; the rest is as on the Analyse tab.
