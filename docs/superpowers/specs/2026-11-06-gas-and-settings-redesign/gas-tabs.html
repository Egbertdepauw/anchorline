One "Gas" destination with three tabs: Blend, Analyse, Log

The bottom-nav "Blend" becomes Gas. Its top bar is one 56 pt row: a small anchor (tap = back to Dives) and the three tabs, so the analyzer fits with the bottom nav showing. Phones are drawn at real iPhone 15 size (59 pt top inset, 34 pt home area). Same dive-screen style throughout.

Blend tabThe fill plan is the summary band at the top and updates as you type below it. Target first, then what's in the cylinder, then the top-off gas. Fill order, real gas and temperatures fold away.
