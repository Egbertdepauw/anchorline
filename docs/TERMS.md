## 3. Safety

**Anchorline is a logbook and an analysis tool. It is not a dive
computer, not a decompression device and not a substitute for your
training, your judgement or your dive team's procedures.**

- The dive computer you dive with is the authority during a dive. What
  Anchorline shows afterwards is a reading of the data that computer
  recorded, and it can be incomplete or wrong, for example because of a
  transfer error, a firmware difference or a bug.
- Cell-health indications, scrubber-time figures, gas-density, END,
  surface-GF, CNS and OTU values are informational. They are computed
  from logged data using published models and your own settings. They do
  not detect every failing cell and do not replace cell checks,
  calibration, pre-dive checks or manufacturer limits.
- The planner and blender produce calculations, not advice. Always verify
  a plan independently, analyse every gas with a calibrated analyser
  before diving it, and dive within your training.
- Diving, and rebreather diving in particular, is dangerous. You dive at
  your own risk. We accept no responsibility for any injury, death or
  loss arising from a dive, whatever Anchorline showed before or after
  it.
